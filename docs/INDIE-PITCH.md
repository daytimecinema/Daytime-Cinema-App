# Partner with the Matinee Film Club
### Fill your daylight seats. Keep your margin.

**The problem you know:** weekday and matinee shows run 15–30% full, and the big
ticketing platforms charge fees that go to *their* bottom line, not yours.

**What we are:** a club for film and theater lovers built around daytime
showings — your emptiest seats are our entire product. Members earn points for
every matinee they attend, collect films like trading cards, play a daily movie
puzzle, and compete on streaks. Then they need somewhere to *go*. That's you.

## What partners get
- **Direct ticket sales, not a middleman.** We connect to the Veezi system you
  already run. Tickets sell in our app, money settles to you, we keep a small
  convenience fee — less than the big platforms charge your customers today.
- **Featured placement** in the club's Indie Partner Program — top billing over
  every chain in the app.
- **Double points on your showtimes** — the loyalty engine pushes members to
  your house specifically.
- **Your matinees, marketed for you:** every member's daily app open shows
  what's playing at your theater before 5 PM.
- **No hardware, no integration project.** You generate one API token in your
  Veezi back office. Ten minutes.

## What it costs
Nothing up front. No monthly fee. We earn only when we put a body in one of
your seats.

## The ask
A 20-minute call and a Veezi API token for a 30-day pilot on matinee
showtimes only. If we don't sell tickets, you've lost nothing.
